# The LinkedIn post

## Post

You ship code every day. Your marketing doesn't.

So I built Marketing Skills: 14 marketing skills for Claude Code, in one open-source plugin.

→ Researches your competitors: SWOT, battlecards, every claim sourced
→ Writes your LinkedIn and X posts, ads, email sequences, and a week of content
→ Makes short videos in 3D and motion graphics, with music, rendered on your own machine
→ Posts nothing until you approve it

This video was rendered with it too: its 3D engine, its renderer, and an original score from its music composer.

Free, MIT licensed, and it works in Claude Code and Codex.

Link in the first comment. What should it make next?
