Look only at the drafted cold email in the reply: its subject line and its body as it would be sent. Ignore the analysis, tables, and notes around it. If there are several drafts, judge the first email to Priya (not a follow-up).

Count the words of the body from the greeting or first line to the last line before the sign-off (the sender's name). Don't count the sign-off, the opt-out sentence, or a mailing address.

PASS if all of these hold: (1) that count is 130 words or fewer; (2) the body asks exactly one thing of the reader (one question or request; a second question that only rephrases the first still counts as one); (3) the subject line is seven words or fewer, doesn't start with "Re:" or "Fwd:", and has no exclamation mark.

FAIL if the body is longer than 130 words, makes two or more separate asks (for example a call and a referral to someone else), the subject breaks any part of (3), or there is no drafted email.
